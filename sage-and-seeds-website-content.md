## 3. What Are Water Lily Pops or Makhana?

### Meet Makhana

Makhana is the seed of the water lily — a traditional Indian food that has been enjoyed for generations.

Cultivated in ponds and lakes, particularly across eastern India, Makhana has a long history in Indian food culture and Ayurveda, where it is regarded as a sattvic food associated with purity and wholesomeness.

For much of the Western world, Makhana is still relatively undiscovered. But that is changing.

Sage & Seeds is here to introduce this humble Indian ingredient to a new audience — bringing its tradition, versatility and satisfying crunch to modern snacking.

## 4. The Story Behind the Snack

### More than a snack. A labour of love.

What looks simple in a packet has a remarkable amount of work behind it.

Makhana is traditionally harvested from water and goes through a careful process of cleaning, drying, roasting and popping before it becomes the delicate, crunchy snack we know today.

The process is labour-intensive and relies on human skill, patience and care.

Even the occasional natural dark mark you may see on a Makhana pop can be part of the traditional popping process.

This isn't just another bag of chips or popcorn.

**It's Makhana — brought to you by people, process and patience.**
